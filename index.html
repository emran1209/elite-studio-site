Our Process

From Concept to Print-Ready Files

Our 3-step digital pre-visualization workflow eliminates print delays and installation mistakes.

Step 01

Vehicle Blueprinting

We match your vehicle make, year, and model against millimeter-accurate body templates to ensure flawless alignment around door handles, trims, and rivets.

Step 02

3D Proofing & Revisions

Review your custom fleet graphics on photorealistic 3D mockups from all angles before sending anything to the print shop.

Step 03

Certified Vector Release

Receive print-ready vector packages (.AI, .EPS, Vector PDF) with installer bleeds universally accepted by wrap installers nationwide.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you print or install the physical vinyl?

We are a dedicated digital design and prepress studio. We deliver certified, millimeter-accurate vector blueprint packages that any local wrap installer or sign shop can print and install immediately.

What file formats do I receive?

You receive high-resolution master vector files (.AI, .EPS, Vector PDF) scaled with proper bleed margins and CMYK color profiles, plus multi-angle 3D visual mockups for proofing.

What is the payment structure?

We require a standard 50% deposit to initiate the 3D blueprint queue, with the remaining 50% balance due upon your final proof approval before vector file transfer.
