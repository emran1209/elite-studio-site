Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our digital prepress deliverables and workflow.

What exact digital files will I receive? +
You receive a complete master production package including scaled vector source files (.AI, .EPS, Vector .PDF) with fonts converted to outlines, CutContour cutlines, multi-angle 3D approval renderings (.PNG/.JPEG), and a 1-page installation spec guide with panel bleed measurements.
Do you physically print or install vinyl on vehicles and signs? +
We are a 100% specialized digital prepress and 3D pre-visualization design studio. We deliver certified, print-ready digital soft copies that any local wrap installer, sign shop, or online commercial trade printer can produce without layout errors.
Can you design matching signage, banners, and yard signs for my brand? +
Yes! We specialize in cohesive commercial packages. Once we engineer your primary branding or vehicle wrap, we translate the exact typography, CMYK color profiles, and logo assets into matching storefront window perf, job-site banners, yard signs, and corporate marketing collateral.
How many design revisions are included? +
Every custom design package includes 2 dedicated rounds of revisions on the 3D digital mockups or architectural signage proofs. We work closely with you until you are 100% satisfied with the visual layout before final vector export.
