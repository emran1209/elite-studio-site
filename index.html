Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our digital design deliverables and workflow.

What exact digital files will I receive? +
You receive a complete master production package including scaled vector source files (.AI, .EPS, Vector .PDF) with fonts converted to outlines, multi-angle 3D approval renderings (.PNG/.JPEG), and a 1-page installation spec guide with panel bleed measurements.
Do you physically print or install vinyl on vehicles? +
We are a specialized digital prepress and 3D pre-visualization design studio. We deliver certified, print-ready digital soft copies that any local wrap installer or commercial print shop can print and install without layout errors.
How many design revisions are included? +
Every custom design package includes 2 dedicated rounds of revisions on the 3D digital mockups. We work closely with you on colors, logo sizing, and typography until you are 100% satisfied with the preview before final vector export.
What types of vehicles do you design for? +
We design for all vehicle classes—commercial cargo vans (Ford Transit, Sprinter, Ram ProMaster), box trucks, pickup fleets, trailers, electric vehicles (Tesla Cybertruck, Model 3/Y), sports cars, and motorcycles.
